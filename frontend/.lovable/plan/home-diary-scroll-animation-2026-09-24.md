# Home diary scroll animation

## Experience
- Remove the girl/person from the Home page while preserving the existing cozy room, palette, navigation, and overall visual style.
- Keep the dimensional open diary as the main visual focus, with stronger page depth, soft shadows, and restrained floating particles.
- Place the existing smart “Get Started” action directly on the open diary pages so it remains readable and clickable once the book opens.

## Scroll sequence
- Expand the Home scene into a smooth scroll-driven story with the diary held in view while the visitor scrolls.
- Open and settle the diary first, then reveal the four existing quotes one at a time with gentle fade-and-slide motion.
- Arrange quotes within and just below the diary so they remain legible without covering the button.
- Provide a stable, fully visible final state for reduced-motion users.

## Responsive behavior
- Tune the diary proportions, quote placement, sticky scene height, and scroll distances for phones, tablets, laptops, and desktops.
- Keep touch scrolling natural and prevent clipping or overlap at short viewport heights.

## Validation
- Confirm the Home animation, in-book button destination, final quote state, reduced-motion behavior, and desktop/mobile layouts.
- Confirm Login, Register, Dashboard, Add Entry, Profile, navigation, authentication, and saved entries remain unchanged.
